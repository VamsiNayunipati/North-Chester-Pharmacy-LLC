Transfer Your Prescriptions

Interested in transferring your prescriptions to North Chester Pharmacy? Our team can guide you through the process.

How Prescription Transfers Work

Transferring an eligible prescription may be easier than you think. Here's how to get started.

01

Contact Our Pharmacy

Call North Chester Pharmacy at (661) 399-3337 or visit us in person to discuss transferring your prescription.

02

Provide Prescription Details

Our pharmacy staff will explain what information is needed, such as your current pharmacy and prescription details.

03

We Coordinate the Transfer

Our pharmacy team can contact your current pharmacy and help coordinate eligible prescription transfers in accordance with applicable requirements.

04

Confirm Your Prescription

Our pharmacy will let you know the next steps, including prescription availability and pickup arrangements.

What You Should Know

Some prescriptions may have transfer restrictions depending on medication type, prescription status, and applicable federal or state laws.

Our pharmacy team can help determine whether your prescription is eligible for transfer.

Prescription Privacy

Please do not send prescription numbers, medication details, insurance information, or other sensitive health information through unsecured website forms or ordinary email.

Contact our pharmacy directly by phone or in person for prescription transfer assistance.
