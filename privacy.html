Privacy Policy

Information about privacy when visiting the North Chester Pharmacy website.

Draft for review: This policy must be checked against the website's actual data collection, hosting arrangements, third-party services, and privacy practices before publication.

1. Introduction

North Chester Pharmacy LLC respects the privacy of visitors to its website. This policy describes how information may be handled when visitors use our website.

2. Information Collected

Depending on how this website is configured, hosting providers and other service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and website access logs.

3. How Information Is Used

Website-related information may be used to maintain website functionality, troubleshoot technical problems, and protect website security.

4. Cookies and External Services

The website may use cookies or external services depending on the features implemented. Any analytics, maps, embedded content, or similar services should be disclosed here before deployment.

5. Prescription and Medical Information

Please do not submit prescription details, medical records, insurance information, or other sensitive health information through unsecured website forms or ordinary website contact channels.

For questions about prescriptions or medications, please contact our pharmacy directly by phone or in person.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to external websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies.

7. Information Security

Appropriate security measures should be implemented for website hosting, maintenance, and any services that process visitor information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

The pharmacy's HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices addresses the use and disclosure of protected health information in connection with pharmacy services.

The pharmacy's current approved Notice of Privacy Practices should be made available separately. A link will be added here once the approved notice is available for publication.

9. Changes to This Policy

This website privacy policy may be updated when website features, service providers, or applicable requirements change.

10. Contact Information

For questions about this website privacy policy, please contact North Chester Pharmacy LLC.
